## Summary

**The feature is verified end to end.** The automated run established that the heuristic detector
behaves correctly on a real page across all nine form shapes, that the negative containment holds
(address form, `membership_expiry` decoy, month/year selects), that the capture plausibility gate
refuses a Luhn-invalid submit while still detecting the field, and that the card gesture round-trip
works with its `isTrusted` guard intact. This run closes the last gap: with the vault unlocked, a
stored card actually fills a live payment form.

That was the defect in issue #152 — cards were storable but inert — and it is now confirmed fixed
against the running app, not only against the unit suite.

**Disposition:** spec promoted to a full pass (steps 1–10). Step 10 will remain operator-only; it
sits behind a human-only vault unlock that exists for good reason, and automating it would require
weakening that boundary.

## Supplement — electron#55048 (link-opened windows use the opener's session and webPreferences)
Goldfinch's popup allow path (`guest-wiring.js`, `action: 'allow'`, no partition key) relies on the popup inheriting the opener's jar.
- **Named-target link clicks never reach the popup branch.** This was tested with synthetic, MCP-trusted and synthetic-shift clicks from `work` and from Burner, plus an **operator's real shift+click** (Flight Director rig, from `work`). Every one opened an ordinary tab. From `work`, the tab stays in `work` with the opener's cookie, no opener handle, an empty `window.name`, and `typeof require` undefined. From Burner, the tab gets a fresh burner, per `inheritFromPartition`. That matches `qualifiesAsPopupRequest`, which requires `disposition === 'new-window'`; link clicks arrive as `foreground-tab`. The surface electron#55048 changes is therefore not reachable from links in Goldfinch.
- **`window.open(url, name, features)` popups (the allow path) keep the opener's session and posture.**
  - Popups from `work` and from Burner carry the opener's cookie marker.
  - Each popup's canvas-fingerprint hash is identical to its opener's: `576fb59a` for `work` and `a356de4e` for Burner. That hash comes from the per-jar farble seed, so it proves the preload ran in the main world. The personal tab (`35787d4d`) is the negative control.
  - Both popups have `typeof require` and `typeof process` undefined, and a live `window.opener`.
  - A burner-opened popup's census `jarId` is `null` by design (`src/main/main.js` `popupTabRows` comment: its partition maps to no registered jar).

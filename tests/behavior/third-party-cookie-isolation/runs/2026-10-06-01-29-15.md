Lessons:
- **Selector:** the `:has(.shield-count)` row selector is wrong when the count is zero, because the element is removed rather than emptied. Use `label.closest('.shield-row')`, or `[data-shield]` once it lands.
- **Panel:** the isolate count disappears immediately on toggle-off, with no restart copy (the Leg 02 target). "Reload to apply" is always visible and could be confused with restart copy.
- **Favicons** never carry cookies, so ignore `/favicon.ico` lines.
- **Session restore** replays every tab about 8 s after relaunch, `/b/set-fp` included. Filter by ts window and use the PID plus re-minted key as the relaunch signal, because wcIds are reused.
- **Cookie list:** the panel's Cookies list shows the whole store (e.g. `[3rd] b_fp`). That is not leakage.
- **Quit:** `appQuit()` always ends with a transport error, and the PID exits in about 0.8 s.

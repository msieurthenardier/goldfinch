## Outcome Assessment

### Objectives Achieved

The flight delivered its objective in full. A fresh profile now boots with neither preference set — the stored row is pinned `version 3` with `homePage` and `searchEngine` both explicitly `null` — and Goldfinch never guesses: `toUrl` returns `null` with no engine, and the last Google coalescing sites (`HOMEPAGE`, `|| 'google'`) are gone, pinned absent by an executable scan. When a choice matters, the tab shows a chrome-owned welcome surface: a **viewless tab record** (`wcId === null`, `tab.welcome = { reasons, pendingQuery }`) whose panel is a child of `#webviews`, with a writable address bar and a working bookmarks bar because it is not an internal page at all (DD1). Typing an address or a bookmark navigates that same tab in place (`attachView`); a search typed with no engine hands off to a welcome tab carrying the query (capped at 2048) and runs it once an engine is chosen; setting the home page from the panel or from Settings attaches the tab. Both preferences are clearable in Settings with unset hints; `homePageCache` is boot-seeded onto the `searchEngineCache` shape (squawk 0005 fixed, not inherited); `openSiteSettingsTab` matches Settings by host instead of hijacking any internal tab; welcome tabs are never snapshotted, never torn off, and are refused by every cross-window move path. Existing installs — pinned explicit by Flight 1 — see nothing change (`search-engine-upgrade` re-run 5/5).

Landed as one flight-end commit (`40dd83d`) on `flight/02-welcome-surface`; PR #167 ready. Unit suite 3716 → 3763 (all passing), typecheck and lint clean. Three new behavior specs authored with the flight (`welcome-home-routing`, `welcome-first-launch`, `welcome-search-handoff`) ran for the first time and were promoted `draft` → `active`; three Flight-1-era specs were re-authored for the new defaults and re-run.

## What Went Well

- **DD1 was the flight's best call and it held completely under live observation.** Making the welcome surface a chrome-owned panel in a viewless tab record — not a fourth internal page — sidestepped the `isInternalTab`/`isInternalPageUrl` audit the mission had named as its principal architectural obstacle. The predicted invariants came for free: writable address bar, bookmarks bar following the jar, in-place navigation, invisibility to `enumerateTabs`, no session or closed-tab capture (observed on disk, not inferred). The mission's original "three-allowlist change" framing was retired as commentary rather than forced through.
- **Design review earned its keep on both legs, one cycle each.** Leg 1: the DD10 predicate as drafted could never have matched a real Settings tab (every path sets a `#privacy` fragment) — and the drafted tests would have masked it; DD8 was *reversed* (existing `wcId`-keyed guards already refuse viewless tabs — make the refusal visible, don't build transfer); `applyToolbarAffordances` was extracted because single-site gating would have left ordinary tabs' buttons dead after their view arrived. Leg 2: the boot barrier awaited `homePage` but not `searchEngine`, and `searchEngineCache` pre-seeded `'google'` — under DD5 a search typed before the seed could have gone to Google without a choice; the empty-input guard; the `navigate` guard ordering (null-engine before welcome-attach, or `attachView(tab, null)` would drop the query). Every fix adopted the reviewer's own recommendation, so no second cycle was needed.
- **FD-run behavior specs caught what no unit test could.** Both fixes at the gate — the missing home-page unset hint and `show()` never settling — were found by the Witnessed runs, not by the Developers' unit passes, because neither `welcome-controller.js` nor `settings.js` has a DOM harness. The gate is exactly where a rendered-pixel asymmetry should be caught; it was, and each fix shipped with a structural test and the fixture was corrected rather than the row soft-pedalled.
- **Squawks 0005 and 0006 closed by observation, as DD4/DD5 predicted.** `search-engine-upgrade`'s failing step 2 passed as authored on the Flight 2 build — a clean confirmation that the cache defect, not the spec, was the cause.
- **Evidence discipline matured.** Before/after `evaluate` probes (tab count, `selectedIndex`, `#address.value`, `welcomeVisible`) were the decisive evidence for every "same tab, count unchanged" row — coincident `example.*` titles made screenshots alone insufficient. Negative claims ("no provider URL ever committed") were carried by diffed `enumerateTabs` snapshots plus a11y greps. The Validator asked mid-run for explicit process attestations on restart-adjacent rows; from then on every such row carried a PID, and step 8's relaunch was corroborated by the re-minted key hash and wcId renumbering.
- **The out-of-band relaunch procedure is now proven four times** (SIGTERM the PID owning the scratch `app.db`, relaunch on the same profile, re-mint, rewrite the crew env, brief the restored topology); the pre-Flight-1 worktree fixture produced an authentic `version 2` row in minutes.
- **Flight-1 debrief recommendations were honored.** The leg design claimed all five inherited items explicitly (Rec. 2); gate entries carried wall-clock beside every count and explicit before → after per leg (Rec. 3 — three flights running now compliant); squawks 0006–0009 were turned around before the acceptance gate so the runs inherited the crew-file apparatus notes (Rec. 4); every absence-asserting scan has a positive control.
- **The deferred-commit model and one flight-end review held.** One Reviewer traced every leg criterion to the diff and its test across ~51 paths, re-ran the suite, and grepped for identity leaks; no blocking findings. Test suite health is flat: +47 tests (+1.3 %, the same shape as Flight 1), ~3.3–4.0 s wall-clock, no skips, no todos, no reproducible flakes.

## What Could Be Improved

### Process

- **The leg-2 premise audit fell one concrete step short.** It reasoned about the re-authored fixtures qualitatively ("the boot tab is a welcome tab") without tracing the *value* of the new derived-state function for the fixture's exact post-setup state — `welcomeReasons(null, null)` returns both reasons, so setting the home page alone correctly leaves the engine block up. Tracing that one return value would have caught the `new-tab-default-routing` fixture error at design time and, with it, the `show()`-never-settles gap. The Flight-1 lesson ("audit against the flight's own text") recurred in a new shape: the flight's *current* DD text was under-specified about background welcome records, and the audit trusted it.
- **DD6 defined the engine's unset hint but not the home page's.** A symmetry check — "for every affordance added to one of a pair of adjacent controls, is the mirror defined?" — is cheap at design time and would have made `welcome-first-launch` checkpoint 3 pass first time.
- **The home-first-then-engine order was reasoned about, never observed.** `submitHome` attaches unconditionally on success even with the engine block still showing (required by DD7's disjunctive exit — setting a home page is itself a navigation), but no spec row exercises that order on a both-unset tab. The Reviewer named it correctly as an awareness note; it should become a row before the next flight touches the surface.
- **Executor transcript loss recurred on `search-engine-preference`** (second run in a row on this spec, after the step-8 report). No state was lost — the closing summary was reconstructed from the Executor's final notes and labeled as such — but the closing summary is now the one artifact the harness can silently drop. Send `[CLOSING]` immediately after the last report rather than after the Validator's final verdict.
- **Two rows are structurally operator-only** (`welcome-search-handoff` 7, `search-engine-preference` 6 — the page-context sheet). Correctly named rather than skipped, and closed by hand at this debrief — but that is now three operator rows across two flights on the same sheet. If a third spec needs the page-context menu, the apparatus (an OS-level input tool, or an allowlisted read of the sheet) becomes a flight-level prerequisite rather than a per-run exception.

## Key Learnings

1. **Sidestepping a conflation beats resolving it.** DD1 avoided the 30-odd-gate `isInternalTab` audit entirely by not making the surface an internal page. The cost — a viewless-record state threading through ~10 guard sites — was bounded, enumerated at design review, and unit-tested per site. When the mission names an "architectural obstacle", ask first whether the feature needs to go through it at all.
2. **Premise audits must trace values, not categories.** "It's still a welcome tab" was true; "it has one reason left" was false. Any new derived-state function a fixture depends on gets its concrete return value traced for the fixture's exact state.
3. **A decision reachable from only some of the paths that can invalidate it is a bug class.** `settle()` fixed one instance; the `render`/`settle`/`show` split is the reusable shape.
4. **The viewless tab record is a general primitive** — a tab that exists but has no content yet, with one constructor and one attach function. The next placeholder surface (onboarding, error recovery) should reuse it rather than re-derive the guard sites.
5. **Live runs catch parity gaps that grep cannot, and grep is what we have.** Until the chrome panel and internal pages have a DOM harness, every affordance pair needs a symmetry check at design time, because the first rendered look at it is the acceptance gate.
6. **Restart claims need a process fact.** Tab-list lineage is inference; a PID (plus the re-minted key hash and wcId renumbering) is evidence.

## Recommendations

1. **[Important] Add the value-tracing step and the paired-control symmetry check to the leg premise audit.** For every re-authored fixture, trace the concrete return value of any new derived-state function (`welcomeReasons`) for the fixture's post-setup state; for every affordance added to one of a pair of adjacent controls, confirm the mirror is specified. Both checks would have prevented both fixes at the gate. Methodology item for the mission debrief.
2. **[Important] Author two behavior-test rows before Flight 3 or the next touch of the surface**: (a) home-first-then-engine on a both-unset tab — `submitHome` attaches and the next new tab offers only the engine block; (b) the burner welcome path — `#welcome-burner-note` renders and a preference set from a burner tab persists app-wide. Both are reasoned-about, never observed.
3. **[Important] Decide the DOM-harness question once, at mission-debrief level.** Four consecutive debriefs have reached the same conclusion; either commission a lightweight harness for `welcome-controller.js` and the `settings.js` IIFEs (a flight, not a squawk) or record grep-shape structural tests as the accepted standard in CLAUDE.md and stop re-litigating it.
4. **[Minor] Fix the `requestTearOff` test overclaim** — call `controller.requestTearOff(tab.id)` directly on a welcome record and assert the no-op. Squawk 0011.
5. **[Minor] Write the apparatus facts above into the behavior-test crew file.** Squawk 0012, same shape as squawk 0008.
6. **[Minor] Flag the `renderer.js` line budget at the start of the next flight that touches it**, and resolve squawk 0003 before a fifth flight adds a settings-write channel.
